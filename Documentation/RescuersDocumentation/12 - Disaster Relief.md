# Disaster Relief

## Purpose

This document explains the humanitarian inspiration behind Rescuers2D and defines the design principles for respectfully representing disaster response, civilians, and affected communities.

## Overview

Rescuers2D is being developed for the Juntos Global Game Jam in support of disaster relief efforts following the Venezuela earthquakes. The project's core design pillars exist specifically to keep the game's tone respectful, hopeful, and rescue-focused rather than exploitative.

## Documentation

### Humanitarian Inspiration

The game is a response to a real humanitarian crisis. Its purpose is to raise awareness of, and support for, disaster relief efforts, using the format of a cooperative puzzle platformer to communicate the value of coordinated emergency response.

> [!note]
> The Lost Vikings' cooperative puzzle-platformer structure was chosen specifically because it models teamwork and complementary skill sets — themes directly relevant to real emergency response operations.

### Why Rescue, Not Violence

- Combat is deliberately minimized and never framed as the primary objective (see [[08 - Enemies]]).
- The core failure and success conditions are built entirely around protecting lives, not defeating opponents (see [[03 - Rules]]).
- Environmental hazards, not human or animal antagonists, are the primary challenge (see [[09 - Hazards]]), keeping the tone focused on survival and cooperation rather than conflict.

### Representing Civilians Respectfully

| Principle | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Dignity | Civilians should be depicted as people in crisis, not helpless props or comic relief. |
| Agency | Where possible, civilian dialogue/animation should reflect resilience, not only distress. |
| Diversity | Civilian character design should reflect the diversity of the real communities affected. |
| No exploitation | Avoid using civilian suffering for shock value, humor, or gratuitous detail. |

### Representing Emergency Responders Respectfully

| Principle | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Character abilities should reflect plausible real-world emergency response roles (firefighting, crowd/hazard protection, technical rescue). |
| Competence | Rescuers should be portrayed as skilled professionals, not caricatures. |
| Teamwork | Reinforce that no single role succeeds alone — consistent with the "Every Rescuer Matters" pillar. |

### Representing Affected Communities Respectfully

- Environmental art should depict disaster damage realistically but avoid gratuitous or graphic detail.
- Level narrative framing should avoid sensationalizing tragedy; focus on the rescue effort and outcome.
- Avoid real, specific, identifiable locations, events, or individuals from the Venezuela earthquakes; use generalized disaster-zone environments instead.

> [!warning]
> All narrative and art content referencing the real-world inspiration must be reviewed against these principles before inclusion. When in doubt, favor understatement over dramatization.

### Design Principles Summary

> [!tip]
> - Rescue is the objective — combat is incidental.
> - Every character and civilian is portrayed with dignity.
> - The disaster setting informs mechanics, not spectacle.
> - The project exists to support real relief efforts, not merely to use them as a backdrop.
